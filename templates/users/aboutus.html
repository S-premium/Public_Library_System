About Us

Dr. Graciano Lopez Jaena Learning Resource Center and Museum — where stories come to life in the heart of Iloilo City.

Who We Are

Our story, our purpose.

The Iloilo City Public Library, also known as the Dr. Graciano Lopez Jaena Learning Resource Center and Museum, is housed at the Graciano Lopez Jaena Shrine on Fajardo Street, Jaro, Iloilo City. The property is held by usufruct for 50 years, turned over by the Dr. Graciano Lopez Jaena Foundation, Inc. to the City of Iloilo to complete and manage the Lopez Jaena Shrine and Library that the Foundation had started.

Named after the renowned Filipino propagandist and journalist, our institution serves as the primary hub for learning, research, and community engagement — providing equitable access to books, digital resources, periodicals, historical artifacts, and inclusive collections that reflect the rich cultural heritage of the Ilonggo people.

Our library is more than just a repository of books. It is a living, breathing space where ideas flourish, communities connect, and the legacy of our past inspires the innovations of our future. We are mandated under Republic Act No. 7743 (June 17, 1994) to provide public library services throughout the Philippines.

Legal Mandate

Republic Act No. 7743 — An Act providing for the establishment of Congressional, City and Municipal Libraries and Barangay Reading Centers throughout the Philippines, appropriating the necessary funds therefor and for other purposes.

Vision, Mission & Quality Policy

The principles that guide everything we do.

Our Vision

The Iloilo City Public Library empowers Ilonggos through lifelong learning and cultural preservation, cultivating information-literate and skilled citizens who actively contribute to a livable, sustainable, and well-governed Iloilo with a robust and inclusive economy.

Our Mission

Provides inclusive and responsive services that empower the Ilonggo community through equitable access to information and resources, preserving our cultural heritage and fostering the development of a livable, sustainable, and resilient city.

Quality Policy
  • Acquire and generate relevant library resources.
  • Implement responsive policies, programs, and initiatives.
  • Employ dynamic technology to deliver products and services to relevant interested parties.
  • Provide efficient, effective, and sustainable services aimed towards the highest satisfaction of its stakeholders.
  • Comply with statutory requirements and continually improve our quality management system.

Our Core Values

The ideals that direct us in service to our community.

Inclusiveness

We create a welcoming environment and provide quality services without bias to gender, socio-economic status, or ethnicity. We believe everyone deserves equitable access to information.

Respect

We treat all library users and staff with respect and dignity in every interaction.

Responsibility

We are responsible for the safekeeping of our resources and ensuring their continued availability for future generations.

Teamwork

We view our patrons as members of our team. We appreciate people for who they are and the significant contributions they make to the organization.

Collaborative

We collaborate to accomplish our mission. We value different points of view and absorb as much knowledge as we can from others.

Communication

Our patrons are the reason the library exists. We encourage open communication through social media, suggestion boxes, and email.

Innovation

We are a learning organization that embraces change and takes appropriate risks to address community needs.

Excellence

We provide up-to-date collections and creative programs for all ages, inculcating a strong sense of responsibility and integrity among all employees.

Passion

We ensure that librarians and libraries provide quality services and resources, and encourage both staff and patrons to enjoy their time in the library.

Library Sections

Explore the diverse collections and spaces available to you.

Periodical Section

Houses magazines, journals, newspapers, and other regularly published materials. Covers a wide range of subjects with both print and online access to keep you informed on current events and research.

General Reference Section

A collection of dictionaries, encyclopedias, atlases, almanacs, and reference books organized alphabetically. Resources here are for room-use only and provide quick, reliable answers across all fields of study.

Circulation Section

Located on the 2nd floor of the building, this section manages the borrowing and returning of library materials. Up to 2 books may be loaned out for 3 days, with renewals available.

Law Books

Contains legal treatises, law journals, law reviews, legal dictionaries, encyclopedias, and various legal reference works arranged by subject — from contract law to international law.

Filipiniana Section

A dedicated space showcasing books and materials about Philippine history, culture, and society — a must-visit area offering a deeper understanding of the country's past, present, and future.

Children's Section

Features storybooks, toys, DVDs, and academic books for kids covering math, science, and more. Walk-in children are also welcomed for personal storytelling sessions.

DICT Tech4ED Center

A Technology for Economic Development center under the eFilipino program of DOST-ICT. It aims to harness ICT to enable, empower, and transform society towards an inclusive, integrated, and equitable countryside.

PWD Section

Features a Braille collection, sensory materials, and educational tools for persons with disabilities — including PWD Math materials and fine motor skill resources for all ages and abilities.

General Guidelines

Rules that help us maintain a safe and productive space for everyone.

  • The library is open Monday–Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, except on holidays.
  • Library users must register by scanning the QR code at the courtesy counter upon entrance.
  • Bags and personal belongings (except valuables) must be deposited at the courtesy counter upon entrance.
  • Cold snacks and lidded drinks are permitted; patrons are responsible for their own garbage.
  • Conversation must be kept at an appropriate and acceptable level.
  • Mobile phones must be set to silent mode. Phone calls must be done outside the library.
  • Library users who wish to use the Internet must register their names in the log-book.
  • Library furnishings, equipment, and materials must be handled with care.
  • Books must be placed on the table after use for library staff to check and shelve.
  • A maximum of two (2) books are allowed for home reading for three (3) days. Books are renewable for loan-out.
  • Books in the General Reference Section are for room-use only. Only Circulation Section books can be loaned out.
  • Anyone found damaging or losing library property is liable for its replacement.
  • Business transactions, meetings, games, Facebook, and non-study-related movie streaming are strictly prohibited.
  • Saving files using flash drives is prohibited to prevent the spread of computer viruses.
  • Only needed pages from a book are allowed to be photocopied.

Fines & Penalties

Policies to ensure fair use of our shared resources.

Overdue

Late Return

₱10.00 / day

A ten-peso charge per day is applied for every overdue book from the Circulation section. Counting covers working days only, excluding holidays.

Lost Book

Lost Materials

Immediately report the loss to the City Librarian and execute an Affidavit of Loss. The book may be replaced with the same copy/edition, the current/updated edition, or a related title as assessed and approved by the City Librarian, subject to Commission on Audit rules.

Tampering

Damaged Materials

Tampered or torn materials will be confiscated by the City Librarian. The borrower must replace the item with the same title, updated edition, or a related subject title as approved by the City Librarian.
