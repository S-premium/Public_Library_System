Library Offerings

  • Free Study or Working Space with Free WiFiThe library provides a conducive environment for learning and work, complete with free WiFi for connectivity.
  • Archives with Historical Materials on the Iloilo ProvinceDelve into the past with the library's extensive collection of historical materials, offering insights into the rich heritage of the Iloilo Province.

Our Vision & Mission

The values that guide everything we do.

Mandate

Republic act no. 7743, june 17, 1994 — An act providing for the establishment of congressional, city and municipal libraries and barangay reading centers throughout the philippines, appropriating the necessary funds therefor and for other purposes.

Vision

The Iloilo City Public Library empowers Ilonggos through lifelong learning and cultural preservation, cultivating information-literate and skilled citizens who actively contribute to a livable, sustainable, and well-governed Iloilo with a robust and inclusive economy.

Mission

Provides inclusive and responsive services that empower the Ilonggo community through equitable access to information and resources, preserving our cultural heritage and fostering the development of a livable, sustainable, and resilient city.

Services & Resources

What we provide for our community.

Readers' Services

  • Free study and work space with WiFi
  • Book Collections: Reference, Filipiniana, General Collection, Rare Books, Childrens' Corner, Fiction, and Periodicals
  • Regular outreach to schools and libraries through literacy programs and book donations

Archives

  • Inventory and disposal of records for Iloilo Provincial Government Offices and district hospitals
  • Digitization and preservation
  • Safekeeping of historical and cultural resources of Iloilo Province
