Iloilo City Public Library — Dr. Graciano Lopez Jaena Learning Resource Center & Museum  ·  Effective January 2024

1. Access & Eligibility

Access to the Iloilo City Public Library Management System is granted exclusively to registered members, library staff, and authorized administrators. By creating an account and logging in, you confirm that:

  • You are at least 13 years of age, or have obtained parental or guardian consent if under 13.
  • All information provided during registration is accurate, current, and complete.
  • You will keep your login credentials strictly confidential and will not share them with any third party.
  • You will notify library staff immediately if you suspect unauthorized use of your account.
  • You understand that account access may be revoked at any time for violation of these terms.

2. Use of Library Resources

All library resources — physical and digital — are made available for educational, research, and recreational purposes only. As a registered user, you agree to:

  • Use borrowed materials responsibly and return them on or before the due date.
  • Pay applicable fines promptly for overdue, lost, or damaged library materials.
  • Refrain from reproducing, distributing, or commercially exploiting any library-owned content.
  • Respect intellectual property rights and applicable copyright laws of the Republic of the Philippines (RA 8293).
  • Not attempt to access, alter, copy, or delete records belonging to other users, staff, or administrators.

3. Privacy & Data Protection

The Iloilo City Public Library is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

  • Your name, email address, and contact details are used exclusively for account management and official communications.
  • Borrowing history is maintained for operational purposes and will not be disclosed to any third party without your explicit consent, unless required by law.
  • All passwords are stored using industry-standard cryptographic hashing (bcrypt) and are never accessible in plain text.
  • You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting library staff.
  • The library will not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any commercial entity.

4. Account Security & Authentication

  • After five (5) consecutive failed login attempts, your account will be automatically locked for fifteen (15) minutes.
  • A One-Time Password (OTP) delivered to your registered email is required to complete every standard login.
  • QR Code login requires an active authenticated mobile session and is one-time-use with a 60-second expiry.
  • Password reset links expire after one (1) hour and are single-use only.
  • You are fully responsible for all actions performed under your account during any active session.
  • Do not access your account on shared, public, or untrusted devices without logging out afterward.

5. Prohibited Conduct

  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to the system, databases, servers, or other users' accounts.
  • Uploading, transmitting, or introducing malicious software, viruses, or any harmful code into the system.
  • Engaging in harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory behavior toward library staff or other users.
  • Using library computers or systems for any activity that is illegal under Philippine law.
  • Misrepresenting your identity, age, or institutional affiliation during registration.
  • Attempting to circumvent or disable any security feature or authentication mechanism of the system.

6. Liability & Disclaimers

The library shall not be held liable for temporary service interruptions, loss of data resulting from user error, or the content of third-party resources accessible through library systems. Use of this system is provided on an "as is" basis.

7. Amendments & Contact

The Iloilo City Public Library reserves the right to revise these Terms & Conditions at any time. Continued use of the system after any amendment constitutes your full acceptance of the revised terms. For questions, contact the Library Administration Office (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
