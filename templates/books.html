How to Become a Member
Follow these steps to get your library card

1
Go to the Iloilo City Public Library

Request a Membership Application Form

3
Fill Out the Application Form
Carefully provide all required personal information accurately and completely.
4
Prepare Membership Requirements
Secure and bring the following:
  • 2 pieces of latest 1×1 ID picture
  • Photocopy of any valid government-issued ID
  • Proof of enrollment or school ID (if applicable)
5
Submit Application and Requirements
Submit the accomplished form together with all required documents to the library staff.
6
Proceed to Waiting Area
After submitting, proceed to the designated waiting area as directed by library staff.
7
Wait for Your Name to be Called
Stay alert and listen for your name for identity verification or card release.
8
🎉 Claim Your Library Membership Card
Receive your official membership card and verify all details.

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

The Iloilo City Public Library is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all library members and visitors.

2. Information We Collect
  • Full name, address, and contact details
  • Membership and borrowing records
  • Transaction history for borrowed and returned materials
  • Event registrations and program participation records
3. How We Use Your Information
  • Managing your library membership account
  • Processing book loans, renewals, and returns
  • Sending notifications about due dates, events, and announcements
  • Improving library services and programs for the community
4. Data Sharing

The library does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data.

6. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data.

Terms of Use

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using the library's facilities, services, and online systems, you agree to these Terms of Use.

2. Library Membership
  • Membership is open to all residents and stakeholders of Iloilo City and Province.
  • Members are responsible for keeping their account information accurate.
  • Membership cards are non-transferable.
3. Borrowing Policies
  • Members may borrow materials for designated loan periods.
  • Borrowed materials must be returned on or before their due dates.
  • Lost or damaged materials must be replaced or paid for.
4. Library Premises and Conduct
  • All patrons are expected to maintain a quiet and respectful environment.
  • Food and beverages are not permitted in reading areas.
5. Online System Use
  • The library's online catalog and member portal are for authorized members only.
  • Unauthorized access is strictly prohibited.
6. Free WiFi and Computers

Provided for educational and informational purposes only. Use for illegal activities is strictly prohibited.
