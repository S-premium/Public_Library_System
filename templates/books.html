How to Become a Member
Follow these steps to get your library card
Step 1 of 812%
1
Go to the Iloilo City Public Library
Visit in person.

Address: Arguelles-Fajardo Street, Jaro, Iloilo City
2
Request a Membership Application Form
Approach the library staff at the front desk and ask for a membership application form.
3
Fill Out the Application Form
Carefully provide all required personal information accurately and completely.
4
Prepare Membership Requirements
Secure and bring the following:
  • 2 pieces of latest 1×1 ID picture
  • Photocopy of any valid government-issued ID
  • Proof of enrollment or school ID (if applicable)
5
Submit Application and Requirements
Submit the accomplished form together with all required documents to the library staff.
6
Proceed to Waiting Area
After submitting, proceed to the designated waiting area as directed by library staff.
7
Wait for Your Name to be Called
Stay alert and listen for your name for identity verification or card release.
8
🎉 Claim Your Library Membership Card
Receive your official membership card and verify all details.

You're now a registered library member!
