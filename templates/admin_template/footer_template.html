Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

The Iloilo City Public Library is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all library members and visitors.

2. Information We Collect
  • Full name, address, and contact details
  • Membership and borrowing records
  • Transaction history for borrowed and returned materials
  • Event registrations and program participation records
3. How We Use Your Information
  • Managing your library membership account
  • Processing book loans, renewals, and returns
  • Sending notifications about due dates, events, and announcements
  • Improving library services and programs for the community
4. Data Sharing

The library does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect your personal data. Access is restricted to authorized library staff only.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, and request the deletion of your personal data held by the library.

Terms of Use

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using the Iloilo City Public Library's facilities, services, and online systems, you agree to comply with these Terms of Use.

2. Library Membership
  • Membership is open to all residents and stakeholders of Iloilo City and Province.
  • Members are responsible for keeping their account information accurate and up to date.
  • Membership cards are non-transferable.
3. Borrowing Policies
  • Members may borrow materials for designated loan periods.
  • Borrowed materials must be returned on or before their due dates.
  • Lost or damaged materials must be replaced or paid for at assessed value.
4. Online System Use
  • The library's online systems are for authorized members only.
  • Unauthorized access or misuse is strictly prohibited.
5. Free WiFi and Computers

Free WiFi is provided for educational purposes only. Use for illegal activities is strictly prohibited.
