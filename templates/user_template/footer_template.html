Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

The Iloilo City Public Library — Dr. Graciano Lopez Jaena Learning Resource Center and Museum — is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all library members and visitors. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you register or use our library services:

  • Full name, address, and contact details (phone number and email address)
  • Membership and borrowing records
  • Transaction history for borrowed and returned materials
  • Event registrations and program participation records
3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Managing your library membership account
  • Processing book loans, renewals, and returns
  • Sending notifications about due dates, events, and announcements
  • Improving library services and programs for the community
4. Data Sharing

The library does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or by authorized government agencies in compliance with applicable Philippine laws, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to your information is restricted to authorized library staff only.

6. Your Rights

As a data subject under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, and request the deletion of your personal data held by the library. For inquiries or requests, please contact us at the information provided on this page.

7. Changes to This Policy

The library reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as necessary. Any significant changes will be communicated through official library announcements.

Terms of Use

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using the Iloilo City Public Library's facilities, services, and online systems, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Use. If you do not agree, please refrain from using our services.

2. Library Membership
  • Membership is open to all residents and stakeholders of Iloilo City and Province.
  • Members are responsible for keeping their account information accurate and up to date.
  • Membership cards are non-transferable and must be presented when borrowing materials.
3. Borrowing Policies
  • Members may borrow materials for designated loan periods as set by library staff.
  • Borrowed materials must be returned on or before their due dates.
  • Members are responsible for the care and safekeeping of borrowed items.
  • Lost or damaged materials must be replaced or paid for at the library's assessed value.
4. Library Premises and Conduct
  • All patrons are expected to maintain a quiet and respectful environment conducive to study and reading.
  • Food and beverages are not permitted in designated reading areas.
  • The library reserves the right to deny access to individuals who violate conduct policies.
  • Personal belongings are the sole responsibility of the patron.
5. Online System Use
  • The library's online catalog and member portal are provided for authorized library members only.
  • Unauthorized access, misuse, or attempts to manipulate the system are strictly prohibited.
  • The library is not liable for any loss or damage resulting from use of online library services.
6. Free WiFi and Computers

Free WiFi and computer access are provided for educational and informational purposes only. Use for illegal activities, accessing inappropriate content, or any activity that violates Philippine laws is strictly prohibited.

7. Amendments

The library reserves the right to amend these Terms of Use at any time. Continued use of library services following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
